Wander at rest counts arm or cursor motion while you imagine nothing, in standard steps per minute.

This is a simulation. The decoder is a statistical model at the accuracy you choose, with outputs that stay correlated from one window to the next and a jaw-clench detector that is right 90% of the time. It is not your EEG. The arm geometry is a placeholder.

The interpreters and their strictness settings are the ones tuned in the Python simulator (sim/) for the nearest of 45, 55, 65, 75 and 85% accuracy. One decoder window every 0.25 s; you see the arm half a second late when the script plays.
